Pots de Creme

Pots de creme is a traditional French dessert featuring a rich baked custard that is chilled and topped with chocolate shavings, whipped cream, or flaky sea salt.

Ingredients

  • 5 egg yolks
  • 5 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup half-and-half
  • 10 ounces bittersweet chocolate, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup very strong hot coffee

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325F
  2. In a medium saucepan, slowly warm up the heavy cream with the chopped chocolate over medium-low heat
  3. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, sugar and salt together for 2 minutes until pale and fluffy
  4. Pour 2 tablespoons of the hot cream into the egg yolk mixture and whisk. Slowly stir in the rest of the hot cream while continuously whisking
  5. Fill 6 small pots or ramekins with the chocolate cream
  6. Place the pots into a large baking dish and fill this dish with hot water 3/4 up the sides of the pots
  7. Bake for 30 minutes, let cool completely before refrigerating
  8. Chill for at least 3 hours before enjoying
